# Perspective epistemics and source honesty

Load this reference before interpreting beliefs, metadata, provenance, sources, or `[[Second Brain Perspective]]`.

## Epistemic layers

Keep these layers distinct in reasoning and language:

1. **Explicit personal commitment:** directly confirmed by the user within a stated scope.
2. **Exploring position:** plausible or attractive to the user but not adopted.
3. **Derived disposition:** synthesized from recurring vault knowledge; characterizes the maintained orientation, not automatically the user.
4. **Source claim:** attributable to a cited author or publication.
5. **Interlocutor judgment:** a fresh conclusion offered by the assistant from the preceding layers.
6. **External supplement:** information obtained outside the vault.

Never say “you believe” when only selection, `stance`, topic frequency, graph structure, an aligned source, or a derived disposition supports the claim.

## Stance

- `adopted` records explicit commitment.
- `aligned` records compatibility without commitment.
- `exploring` records consideration.
- `contested` records material disagreement or unresolved conflict.
- `reference` records useful context without a position.

Read content and provenance; metadata is retrieval support, not evidence by itself.

## Perspective use

Read `Knowledge/Second Brain Perspective.md` completely for perspective-guided counsel. Retrieve the relevant underlying notes rather than treating the Perspective as self-justifying. Give explicit commitments priority within their scope, then derived dispositions and exploring positions. Preserve recorded conflicts, exceptions, and boundaries.

An interview can produce candidate beliefs, priorities, red lines, exceptions, or tensions. It cannot establish them as personal perspective until the user confirms the proposed wording or substance. Persistence belongs to `second-brain-librarian`.

## Sources and currentness

Never claim an external source was read when only its vault note was read. `source_checked` is a historical verification date, not proof of present authority or fitness. For consequential or time-sensitive advice, verify live against official or primary sources, identify external supplements, and disclose access limits and currentness.
