# Maintain the Second Brain Perspective

After every operation that creates, edits, moves, merges, or removes a note in `Knowledge/`, evaluate whether it can materially affect the maintained vault orientation. Do not read the full `Knowledge/Second Brain Perspective.md` by default.

## Impact gate

Perform a full Perspective review when the operation:

- adds or changes explicitly recorded `## Personal perspective`;
- changes `stance` in a way relevant to the maintained orientation;
- edits, moves, merges, or removes a note already used or cited by the Perspective;
- adds material evidence for a recurring disposition, counterevidence, tension, scope boundary, or confidence change; or
- explicitly targets the Perspective or the maintained orientation.

When any trigger applies, read `Knowledge/Second Brain Perspective.md` completely before completing the operation. Changes made during that review do not trigger another review. Routine factual additions, typo fixes, and edits to unrelated concepts do not trigger a full read.

- Treat it as the maintained, provisional orientation reconstructed from recurring vault knowledge plus explicitly recorded personal perspective, neither an ordinary synthesis nor automatically the user's beliefs.
- Edit it only for a material change to a recurring disposition, supporting evidence, tension, scope, confidence, or explicitly recorded personal perspective.
- Preserve distinct sections for explicit personal commitments, exploring positions, derived dispositions, moral priorities, decision heuristics, tensions, scope boundaries, and revision provenance. Omit an empty layer rather than inventing content.
- Treat an interview inference as a candidate until the user explicitly confirms its substance; selection, continued discussion, or lack of objection is insufficient.
- Keep conversational character and challenge style in the Interlocutor skill, not in the canonical Perspective. A communication preference is not automatically a moral belief.
- Keep it concise and provisional, integrating counterevidence rather than only confirmation.

## Counterevidence and underrepresented perspectives

Treat a deliberate search for counterevidence or underrepresented perspectives as a separate review path. Run it when explicitly requested or when a broader vault review makes it useful; do not impose a cadence or run it solely because a Knowledge mutation occurred. Record material gaps and disagreements without manufacturing balance. If its findings target or materially affect the maintained orientation, apply the impact gate and review the Perspective.

## Completion report

State exactly one outcome, followed by the reason:

- `Perspective reviewed: updated`
- `Perspective reviewed: no change`
- `Perspective review not triggered`
