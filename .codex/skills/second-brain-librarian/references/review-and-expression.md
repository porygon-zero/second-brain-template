# Review and expression

Load this reference for vault review, critique with changes, distilling toward output, or saving reusable synthesis.

## Express

- Infer or ask for audience, outcome, and format.
- Retrieve relevant vault notes before external research.
- Identify agreements, tensions, gaps, and distinctive explicitly recorded personal perspective.
- Preserve whether a draft came from neutral expert synthesis or perspective-shaped counsel; writing style does not change the epistemic status of its claims.
- Never convert aligned, exploring, selected, or derived material into the user's first-person conviction without explicit confirmation.
- Produce the smallest useful intermediate packet: a decision aid, explanation, checklist, outline, comparison, or other concrete result.
- Save synthesis only when it will be reusable beyond the immediate task.

## Review

Review for usefulness, not tidiness. Identify notes that support an active output, recurring ideas needing a summary, productive tensions, missing bridges, and material collected without a use. Improve frequently used knowledge first; do not require processing every note.

When reviewing classification, assess poorly fitting notes, recurring clusters, overlapping or overly broad domains, and missing bridges. Run `scripts/analyze-communities` when wikilink topology can provide useful diagnostic evidence, then read the implicated notes rather than interpreting the graph alone. Use `--publish` when completing a requested visual/latest refresh or when a substantial relationship or domain batch has materially stale published results. A review may offer a domain proposal only under the proposal contract in [domain-maps-and-index.md](domain-maps-and-index.md); it must not create or register one without approval.

Do not reorganize the vault for theoretical purity. Keep evidence limitations and uncertainty visible.
